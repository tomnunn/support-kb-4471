Counted from the export at render time: one report per id, placed by what its tenant runs, not by its subject or its intake tag. Intake tags come from the reporter’s browser; a tenant’s people often book from an en-US browser, so the tag is wrong in both directions.

parser: the shared parser reads day-first dates month first

Every tenant writes the day first; the parser reads the month first. Day and month swap: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July” (R-41200), “Booked for 2 December. The invite says 12 February” (R-41203). It comes in through the app, by email and through booking-v2 forms, from every tenant. This is the fix that is already queued.

hint: booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to type the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. A day-first tenant’s people type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The result looks the same as the parser bug, a swapped day and month, but only on booking-v3 forms (R-41241, R-41305, R-41387). The parser fix does not touch it: the hint has to follow the tenant’s locale. The desk’s own table already put these apart and marked them “no ticket of its own”.

one-day: bookings move by a single day

No swap. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May” (R-41202). “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday” (R-41620). “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting” (R-41344). “The invite arrived before I made the booking” (R-41236). “Every booking in June is one day off” (R-41209). That is a time zone or day-boundary bug, not date order. The desk noted these reports (desk-notes.json) but counted them under parser, so the parser fix would have been expected to close them.
